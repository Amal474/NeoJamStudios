PathFinder Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 Sep 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NeoJamStudios (“we,” “our,” or “us”) handles information when you play our 2D game PathFinder (the “Game”).

1. Data Collection

a. Personal Data

We do not collect, store, or process personal data such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account information.

b. Game Data

The Game itself does not collect or store gameplay data such as chat messages, player-created content, or user accounts.

2. Unity Ads and Analytics

The Game uses Unity Ads, a service provided by Unity Technologies, to display advertisements.

Unity Ads may automatically collect certain non-personal and technical information, which may include:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system and version
  • Advertising identifiers (such as IDFA or AAID, where permitted)
  • IP address (processed in anonymized or aggregated form)
  • Ad interaction data (such as impressions and clicks)
  • Gameplay session data and performance metrics

Unity Ads may also use this information for analytics purposes, including measuring ad performance, detecting fraud, and improving services.

This data is collected and processed by Unity Technologies in accordance with their own Privacy Policy. We do not have direct access to personally identifiable information collected by Unity Ads.

Unity Privacy Policy:
https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

3. How the Information Is Used

Information collected through Unity Ads and its analytics is used to:

  • Display relevant advertisements
  • Measure ad performance and effectiveness
  • Detect fraud and ensure ad integrity
  • Improve game stability and user experience

We do not sell or knowingly share user data ourselves.

4. Children’s Privacy

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Unity Ads is responsible for handling any data it collects in compliance with applicable child protection laws.

If you believe that personal data has been collected unintentionally, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

5. Data Security

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Any data collected through Unity Ads is secured and managed by Unity Technologies in accordance with their security practices.

6. Your Choices

Depending on your location, you may be able to limit or opt out of personalized advertising through:

  • Your device’s advertising and privacy settings
  • Unity’s privacy controls

Please refer to Unity’s Privacy Policy for detailed information on your rights and choices.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the “Last updated” date will be revised accordingly.
